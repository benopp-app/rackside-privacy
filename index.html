Privacy Policy

Effective 3 August 2026 · Last updated 3 August 2026

The short version: Rackside does not collect, transmit, or sell your data. It has no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising. Everything you enter stays on your device.

No data collection No accounts No tracking No ads

1. Who this covers

This policy applies to the Rackside mobile application ("Rackside", "the app") for iOS and Android. Rackside is an offline reference and calculation toolkit for IT technicians.

2. Information we collect

We collect nothing. Rackside has no servers and no backend. It does not create an account, does not ask you to sign in, and does not send your information anywhere.

Specifically, Rackside does not collect:

  • Personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number
  • Device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or location data
  • Usage analytics, telemetry, crash reports, or diagnostics
  • The contents of anything you type into the app

The app contains no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking software of any kind.

3. Information stored on your device

Some features save data locally so they work between launches. This data never leaves your device, is not readable by other apps, and is not accessible to us.

  • Certificate Tracker. Entries you create — name or domain, expiry date, and any optional issuer, location, owner, and notes — are stored in the app's private storage.
  • Bundled reference databases. Port, MAC vendor, and error-code databases ship inside the app and are copied into its private storage on first launch. These are read-only reference data and contain nothing about you.

4. Permissions

  • Notifications (optional). If you allow notifications, Rackside schedules local reminders for certificate expiry dates you have entered. These are generated on your device by the operating system. Rackside does not use push notifications, does not register a push token, and no server is involved. Declining notifications disables reminders; every other feature continues to work.

Rackside does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or files.

5. Network access

Rackside performs no network requests in normal operation. All lookups and calculations run entirely on your device against data bundled with the app. The app is designed to be fully functional with no connectivity.

Two actions you initiate can leave the app:

  • Copy and Share. When you use a Copy or Share button, the selected result is placed on your clipboard or handed to the app you pick. What happens next is governed by that app's own privacy policy.
  • Links. Tapping a link in Settings (this policy, a data-source reference, or the support email) opens your browser or mail app.

6. Third parties

Rackside integrates no third-party analytics, advertising, or data-sharing services. We share no data with anyone, because we do not have any.

The app is distributed through the Apple App Store. Apple may independently collect information such as download counts and, if you have enabled sharing of analytics and crash data in your device settings, crash diagnostics. That collection is Apple's, governed by Apple's Privacy Policy, and is outside our control. We do not receive personally identifying information from it.

7. Data retention and deletion

Because your data is stored only on your device, you control it entirely. Delete individual certificate entries inside the app at any time. Uninstalling Rackside permanently removes all of its stored data from your device. We hold no copy and cannot recover it.

8. Children's privacy

Rackside is a professional tool intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

9. Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export, and delete personal data a company holds about you. Rackside holds no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, or erase. Data stored locally is under your direct control as described in section 7. We do not sell or share personal information, as those terms are defined under the CCPA.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a revised "Last updated" date. Material changes to how the app handles data will accompany an app update, and the change will be described here.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or the app's privacy practices: support@rackside.us
